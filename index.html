❓ How to Play

Move: ← →   Jump: ↑   Shoot: Space   Shield: Shift / E   Dash: X

Every attack has a wind-up. Watch where the boss is heading, glowing or aiming and look for markers on the floor - then walk, dash or jump out of the way. Low shockwaves and crawling enemies can be jumped over.

Your shield blocks a hit and slowly recharges over time. Dash gives a burst of speed and brief invincibility on a cooldown.

Bosses wear themselves out after their biggest moves - pay attention to how they act.

Bring the boss's HP bar at the top of the screen to zero before your team runs out of hearts!
